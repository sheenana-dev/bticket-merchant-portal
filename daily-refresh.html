How Daily Refresh Works

Consumer

"Available Today" vs "Used Today" Split

My Vouchers is now split into two clear sections. Available coupons are on top with "Use Now →". Used ones are faded with a refresh countdown showing exactly when they'll be back.

Consumer

Frequency Badges

Each card shows a colored badge: "Daily" (green), "Weekly" (blue), "One-Time" (red). Users instantly know which coupons refresh and which are use-it-or-lose-it.

Consumer

Refresh Countdown Bar

"Refreshes tomorrow at 6:00 AM · in 12 hours" — gives the user a reason to open the app first thing tomorrow morning. Drives daily habit.

Consumer

"Today's Deals" Banner

Shows the date, refresh time, and count of available coupons. Creates a "daily drop" feeling — like checking what's new each morning.

Merchant

Frequency Control

Merchants choose: daily, weekly, monthly, or one-time. A milk tea shop picks "daily." A spa picks "monthly." Each merchant controls their own exposure.

Merchant

Daily Cap Protection

"Max 30 per day" prevents a viral coupon from overwhelming the kitchen. When the cap hits, the coupon shows "Sold out for today" — creating scarcity AND protecting the merchant.

Merchant

Custom Refresh Time

Default 6:00 AM, but merchants can change it. A bar might set refresh at 5:00 PM for happy hour deals. Aligns with actual business hours.

Merchant

Live Preview

Before saving, the merchant sees exactly how the rules will appear to customers. No guessing — reduces support tickets and merchant confusion.
