What Changed & Why

Removed

"Discounted price" label

Database field language. Replaced with "Pay at store" — tells the user exactly what this number means.

Added

"Pay at store" label

Clarifies that ₱450 is what you pay at the merchant, not in the app. Removes confusion about whether collecting costs money.

Added

50% OFF badge

Users process percentages faster than doing ₱900 minus ₱450 in their head. The coral color draws the eye immediately.

Added

"Free to collect" banner

Reinforces the subscription value. The green checkmark + banner makes it clear: you're not paying anything in the app.

Removed

Tiny strikethrough price

₱900 was hidden as small gray text. Now it's the same size as ₱450, side by side, so the comparison is instant.

Changed

"Collect" → "Collect Coupon"

Slightly more descriptive. Combined with the "Free to collect" banner, there's zero ambiguity about what tapping does.
