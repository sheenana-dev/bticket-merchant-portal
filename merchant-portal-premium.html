Frequently Asked Questions
How do I create my first coupon?
+
Go to Create Coupon from the sidebar. Fill in basic details (title, discount, price), upload photos, set redemption rules, then submit for review. Our team approves within 24 hours.
How does the QR scanning work?
+
When a customer visits your store, they show their coupon QR code. Your staff scans it using the B-ticket Scanner App (available on iOS and Android). The redemption is instantly recorded.
Is listing on B-ticket really free?
+
Yes! Creating and listing coupons is completely free. There are no subscription fees, commissions, or hidden charges. You only pay if you choose optional in-app advertising.
How long does coupon approval take?
+
Our team reviews and approves coupons within 24 hours (usually faster during business hours). You'll get an email and notification when approved.
Can I pause or stop a coupon?
+
Yes. Go to My Coupons, find the coupon, and click Pause. It will be hidden from customers immediately. You can resume it anytime.
What is the daily cap?
+
The daily cap limits how many times a coupon can be redeemed per day. Once reached, the coupon shows “Sold out for today” and resets at your chosen time (default 6AM).
