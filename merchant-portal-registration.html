Merchant Agreement
Please review and accept the B-Ticket Merchant Terms of Service.

B-TICKET MERCHANT TERMS OF SERVICE

This Merchant Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between B-Ticket Technologies, Inc. ("B-Ticket") and the Merchant identified in the registration form ("Merchant"), collectively referred to as the "Parties."

1. Platform Services

B-Ticket provides a digital coupon marketplace platform connecting Merchants with consumers. Merchants may create, publish, and manage digital coupons through the B-Ticket Merchant Portal.

2. Commission Structure

B-Ticket shall collect a commission on each coupon redeemed, as follows: (a) Free Plan: 15% commission per redemption; (b) Starter Plan: 12% commission; (c) Pro Plan: 8% commission; (d) Enterprise Plan: Custom rate as agreed.

3. Payment Terms

Settlements shall be processed bi-monthly (every 15th and 30th). Payments will be remitted to the Merchant's registered bank account within 3 business days of settlement date. Minimum payout threshold is ₱500.

4. Merchant Obligations

The Merchant agrees to: (a) honor all valid coupons presented by consumers; (b) maintain accurate business information; (c) comply with all applicable Philippine laws including the Consumer Act (RA 7394), Data Privacy Act (RA 10173), and BIR regulations.

5. Intellectual Property

Each Party retains ownership of its respective intellectual property. Merchant grants B-Ticket a non-exclusive license to use Merchant's name, logo, and images for platform display and promotional purposes.

6. Data Privacy

Both Parties agree to comply with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173). Consumer data collected through the platform shall be processed in accordance with B-Ticket's Privacy Policy.

7. Term and Termination

This Agreement is effective upon approval and continues until terminated. Either party may terminate with 30 days written notice. Outstanding obligations survive termination.
